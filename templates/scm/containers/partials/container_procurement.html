{% load i18n %} {% comment %} Procurement: the two things purchase orders say about this physical box. "Acquired through" is where the box came from — the order line that bought it, so a 45ft equipment line reads as the line that produced this ISO number. "Contents" is what it is carrying — the purchased lines whose goods are loaded in it, with the quantity where somebody recorded one. Two different claims, kept under two headings and never merged. A flat list of purchase orders against a container cannot distinguish "this box is the purchase" from "this box holds the purchase", and an operator who cannot tell them apart cannot act on either. Built by `apps.scm.procurement.container_links.get_container_procurement` and held on the workspace, so nothing is decided here. Absent entirely for a box with no procurement link: an unlinked container should read as a shorter page, not as a panel with two empty headings. A bulk-built workspace has no procurement at all (see ContainerWorkspace) and is treated the same way rather than reported as none. {% endcomment %} {% with procurement=workspace.procurement %} {% if procurement and procurement.has_any %}
