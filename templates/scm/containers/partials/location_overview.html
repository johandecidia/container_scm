An absent status renders no row rather than a zero — a depot holding nothing but available boxes should not display four empty counters. Expected arrivals get a panel here rather than being hidden, because "nothing is expected" and "we cannot tell you what is expected" are different answers and the first one is now the true one: the set comes from canonical destinations, so an empty list means nothing is routed here rather than that the question is unanswerable. Since LOC-4 the place itself is on a map when its coordinates are recorded, and says so plainly when they are not. One marker for the location, carrying the inventory count — not one per container, because they all share the location's single coordinate and eighty stacked discs would say nothing eighty times. The hierarchy panel sits below them. It is on the Overview rather than behind a tab of its own because containment is part of what this place *is* — the same category as its code and its type — and because it is the panel somebody arrives at from the data-quality queue. {% endcomment %}
