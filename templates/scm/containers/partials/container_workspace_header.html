{% load i18n %} {% comment %} The container workspace header: who this box is, what state it is in, and what can be done about it. Everything here is derived from the workspace read model. Nothing is a placeholder — a container with no supplier, no route or no ETA renders a shorter header rather than a header full of dashes, so the shape of it says how much is known. Three different statuses stay apart, as they do in the read model: the container's own business status is the badge beside the number, the carrier's last observation is the current-state line inside Overview, and how current the tracking is has its own line here. This is the reference header for the workspaces that follow — shipment, purchase order, location. The frame is: identity, type, business context, route, ETA, source freshness, actions. {% endcomment %}
