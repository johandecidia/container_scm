{% load i18n %} {% comment %} Order progress, as six numbers and one bar. The numbers come from the existing fulfillment engine and one aggregate over the supplier delivery lines. Nothing is stored: change a delivery line and these change on the next read. "Assigned" is quantity booked onto a supplier delivery, whether or not that delivery names a container yet. It is a planning act and is kept apart from "Shipped", which is a physical one — collapsing them would make an order that is merely planned look like an order that has left. The bar is three stacked segments over the ordered quantity — received, arrived, shipped — widest last so the shorter ones stay visible. No chart library: it is three divs. Grid, not a table, so it reflows to two columns on a phone instead of scrolling. {% endcomment %} {% if workspace.has_progress %}
