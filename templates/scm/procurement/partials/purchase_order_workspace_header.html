{% load i18n %} {% comment %} The purchase order workspace header: which order, whose goods, and how far along it actually is. The badge beside the number is the SCM *logistics* status — computed from the fulfillment quantities, and the only status an operator can act on. The Business Central *document* status is a different fact about a different thing, so it sits in Order details on the Overview tab with the rest of the source metadata rather than competing here. Conflating the two is the specific mistake this header exists to stop. The frame is the container workspace's: identity, status, business context, dates, source, progress, actions. {% endcomment %}
